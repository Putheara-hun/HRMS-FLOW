09 The numbers

So two people building different screens end up with the same thing.

TokenValueWhere
Field height48px minBelow 44px a thumb misses it. Textareas grow from here.
Corner radius14px fields · 999px chipsCards 17px, sheets 22px, phone 46px.
Border1.5px #e3e9f2Brand on focus, red on error, green when validated.
Focus ring4px at 14% opacityOutside the border, never replacing it — colour alone is not enough.
Label.68rem · 750 · uppercaseAbove the field. Never a placeholder standing in for a label.
Help / error.70rem, one lineError replaces help in the same slot, so nothing shifts.
Gap between fields14px22px between groups.
Tap target44px minimumApplies to the ✕ in a chip and the toggle, not just buttons.
Required markRed * on the labelMarked on required, not on optional — most fields are required.
Two rules that are not about pixels. A placeholder is never a label — it vanishes the moment someone types, and they lose track of which box they are in. And validate when a field loses focus, not on every keystroke: turning a field red while somebody is still halfway through typing their own name is the fastest way to make an app feel hostile.
Component design only. Nine groups, six states, one spec table — everything the HRMS mobile screens are assembled from.
